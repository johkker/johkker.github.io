# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
The site is the public face of the RapTrak app (https://app.raptrak.me), for the rap battle scene in Brazil:
- **Fan/spectator**: finds out what RapTrak is, where to use it, and how to follow battles near them. Arrives from Instagram, WhatsApp, or a battle flyer, mostly on a phone at night.
- **MC**: wants to know whether their wins and trophies will be recorded and shareable.
- **Organizer**: needs to be convinced that putting their battle on the map is free and worth it. The `/cena` page is written for them.
- **Stores and legal reviewers**: the privacy, data-deletion, and child-safety pages must stay reachable (Play Store compliance).

## Product Purpose
Explain the app in one screen, send people to the web app or the Android beta, and win over the scene (organizers, MCs, public). Success means someone who opens the link understands in 5 seconds what it is, feels it was made for the scene, and taps "Abrir o app". No pitch to brands here yet; that comes once there are usage numbers.

## Brand Personality
Raw, competitive, street culture, energetic, and **executed with care**, never amateur. Informal, direct PT-BR, with scene slang used sparingly. Three words: **poster, roda, registro**.

## Anti-references
- The site's own previous version and the app's old version: violet-to-cyan gradient, glassmorphism, purple glow, Inter/Space Grotesk, neon, hard brutalist shadows, pixel or marker fonts.
- AI slop: cream or beige background, identical icon cards in a grid, an eyebrow above every section, gradient text.
- Soulless corporate SaaS, and "startup landing page" with fake metrics.

## Design Principles
- **Same DNA as the app.** Whoever lands on the site and opens the app sees the same brand: Editorial Street.
- **The scene is the visual.** Real app screenshots and real battles, never invented decoration.
- **Tell the truth about the moment.** Beta on Android, web live, iPhone coming. Never promise a feature as ready when it isn't.
- **Free for the scene.** Sponsorship shows up as mutual help (drops, discounts, giveaways). The site never frames it as brands paying the app.
- **Teach how to use it.** "Como funciona" is a real sequence of steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA as the floor: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, touch targets ≥ 44px, visible focus, respect for reduced-motion. Legibility over elegant light gray.
